Mashpee: Our Home Base

The business address is 16-38 Nathan Ellis Hwy, Mashpee, MA 02649, near Mashpee Commons at the Rotary where Routes 28 and 151 meet. Mashpee is bordered by Sandwich to the north and northwest, Barnstable to the east, Falmouth to the west and Nantucket Sound to the south.

Water enters Mashpee homes in all the ways it does elsewhere: plumbing, appliances, roofs and basements. It also arrives with coastal storms, which the town records in its hazard plan. Mashpee also has an active sewer project, so some neighborhoods are moving from septic to sewer over time while others stay on septic.

For emergency work, see emergency water removal. For storm and flood losses, see flood damage restoration.

Why Each Town Has Its Own Page

A page about Falmouth that could be about Cotuit by changing the name would not help anyone. Each town page uses its own flood history, utility, housing and landmarks, because those facts change what a water loss looks like and what a homeowner should check first.

For example, Falmouth and East Falmouth sit along Buzzards Bay and Vineyard Sound with a documented history of coastal flooding. Cotuit and Osterville face Nantucket Sound. Marstons Mills is inland, with kettle ponds, a town-owned dam and cranberry bogs.

Mashpee Storm History, in Brief

The town's 2017 Hazard Mitigation Plan records Hurricane Carol in late August and early September 1954 flooding Shore Drive, and Hurricane Bob on August 19, 1991 flooding the causeway near Amy Brown Road. The National Weather Service puts Bob's surge at 12 to 15 feet at Mashpee and a few neighboring Buzzards Bay towns.

The plan also describes the January 24, 2005 blizzard as causing coastal flooding with major damage to homes in vulnerable areas. For a homeowner, the lesson is practical. Know your flood zone, photograph the house while it is dry and keep the insurer's phone number where you can find it at night.

Public Offices Worth Knowing

The Mashpee Water District is an independent municipal entity that handles connection permits and the public supply. The town Building Department handles permits for rebuilding after a loss. Fifteen FEMA flood map panels cover Mashpee, and the town explains how to look up a parcel on its flood pages.

Cotuit and Osterville homes are served by separate water districts, and the numbers for those are on the town pages. If a main breaks in the street, the district is the first call. If water is inside your house, ours is.

How To Pick the Right Page

Start with where the house sits. Shoreline and bay-front streets in Falmouth, East Falmouth, Cotuit and Osterville raise storm and tide questions. Inland streets, including much of Marstons Mills, are more often plumbing, roof and rain problems. The page for your town explains which applies, and the service pages explain what each stage of the work involves.
