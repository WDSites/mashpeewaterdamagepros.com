Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Mashpee, MA

A frozen pipe often does its damage during the thaw, when the ice holding a split closed melts and water pours out. If a tap has stopped running, act before the ice goes.

Why Pipes Freeze on a Coast That Feels Mild

Water expands as it freezes, and ice in a pipe traps water between the blockage and a closed faucet. Pressure builds there, and the pipe splits, often away from the ice itself. The split may stay shut while frozen and open the moment it thaws.

Cape winters feel mild, but pipes freeze anyway. The usual trouble spots are pipes in exterior walls, runs in an unheated crawl space or garage, lines over a cold porch and hose faucets outside.

What the Numbers Say About a Cape Winter

The Massachusetts residential building code lists a frost depth of 48 inches, so frost reaches that far into the ground. Anything exposed near the foundation, such as a crawl space line, feels the cold long before the rooms do.

NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals for the Hyannis airport station show how long it lasts. About 24.7 days in January have a low at or below 32 degrees, and the count is 23.1 in February, 21.0 in December and 19.2 in March.

A normal low is an average night, so real cold snaps run colder. A weakly protected wall can face freezing on most nights for four months.

Signs a Pipe Is Frozen or Already Split

  • A tap that trickles or runs dry. Ice is blocking the line, so leave the tap open.
  • Frost on a visible pipe, or a bulge in it. The metal is stretched and may be split.
  • A damp spot or running sound after a warm-up. The thaw has opened a split, so close the main.
  • A ceiling or crawl space drip after a cold night. The wet area is often below the break, not at it.

Safety Steps Before and During a Thaw

  1. If the tap is dry, open it and shut the main valve. A closed main means a hidden split cannot pour into the house when the ice melts.
  2. Never use a torch or open flame to thaw a pipe. A hair dryer is gentler, but only when no water is on the floor and the cord stays dry.
  3. If water is already running, shut the main, open a low faucet and keep out of any room where water touches outlets or the panel.
  4. For gas, sparking or a hot smell at the panel, leave and call 911 and the utility.
  5. Call a licensed plumber for the pipe and the 24/7 line for pumping and drying. Photograph the frozen section.

Thaw starting and water showing?

Close the main, then call. Wet insulation and subfloor are far easier to dry on day one than on day three.

How We Dry a House After a Winter Split

  1. Confirm the water is off and check for water near wiring.
  2. Remove standing water with submersible pumps and extraction units.
  3. Follow the water. In a cold house it often runs through insulation into the floor system, so a thermal imaging camera and meter readings map the route.
  4. Remove what will not dry. Soaked insulation, sagged ceiling drywall and wet carpet pad come out.
  5. Warm and dry. Cold materials dry slowly, so air movers and dehumidifiers run alongside safe heat in the rooms.
  6. Monitor daily until readings reach the drying goal.

Cold-weather drying is slow work, and the method is laid out in our drying guide. A split in a pressurized line is also covered under burst pipe water damage.

Heat, Occupancy and Your Insurer

Mass.gov, in guidance from the Office of Consumer Affairs and Business Regulation, says to keep the home adequately heated in extreme cold. It adds that burst-pipe damage is generally covered only if the home was properly heated and not left unoccupied.

A separate mass.gov winter guide says frozen pipes may not be covered if damage comes from negligence, such as not maintaining adequate heat. If you close up a Cape house for part of the winter, read your policy and ask what your insurer expects.

What Changes the Cost After a Freeze

  • Whether the split was in an exterior wall, crawl space, garage or finished room.
  • How much insulation, drywall and flooring is wet.
  • How many days cold materials keep equipment on site.

Prevention From the State's Winter Advice

Mass.gov tells homeowners to insulate and maintain pipes, seal leaks or cracks around them, and shut off and cover exterior faucets used for garden hoses.

In practice, disconnect hoses in the fall, insulate lines in the crawl space and garage, and open the cabinet doors under a sink on an outside wall during a freeze. If you will be away, ask a neighbor to check the house, because heat that fails while no one is home is the costly case.

Common Questions

Is emergency frozen pipe water damage restoration available in Mashpee?

A dispatcher takes calls all night. On a freezing night the crew checks where thaw water is running, pumps out what has pooled and traces moisture behind exterior walls before setting dryers. Cold materials dry slowly, so equipment goes in early, with no arrival time promised.

How cold does it get in Mashpee in winter?

NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals for the Hyannis airport station give a January normal low of 24.1 degrees. Real cold snaps run colder than that average, and about 24.7 January days dip to freezing or below.

Should I shut the water off if a pipe is frozen but not leaking?

Yes, closing the main is a safe precaution. A split can hide behind ice and begin to pour once the pipe thaws. Leave the affected tap open so water has somewhere to go.

Is frozen pipe damage covered by insurance?

Mass.gov says burst-pipe damage is generally covered only if the home was properly heated and not left unoccupied, and that negligence may void coverage. Read your policy and ask your insurer.

Can I thaw the pipe myself?

Only with gentle warmth, such as a hair dryer, and only when the floor is dry. Never use a torch. If you see a bulge or a wet spot, shut the main and call a plumber.
