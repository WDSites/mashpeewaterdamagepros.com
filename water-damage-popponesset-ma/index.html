Water Damage Cleanup in Popponesset, Mashpee, MA

Popponesset sits on the bay of the same name on Mashpee's south shore. Storm tide and wind-driven rain are the realistic risks here, along with leaks in homes that fill and empty with the seasons.

Where Popponesset Is

Popponesset lies on Popponesset Bay in Mashpee, next to New Seabury. The Census Bureau lists Popponesset and Popponesset Island as separate places, and the town's hazard plan labels Popponesset Beach and Popponesset Bay on its maps. We describe the area only that way, because no source gives official street boundaries.

Water here usually comes from one of two directions: the bay and the sky, or the plumbing inside. The response differs for each, so the first call should say which one you think it is.

Tide, Storms and What the Record Shows

FEMA's countywide flood insurance study, effective July 16, 2014, says Nantucket Sound tidal flooding affects Popponesset Bay. That is a statement about the bay shore, not a map of any single street.

CBS Boston reported on a July 2019 storm in Mashpee where a family sheltered in the basement of a Popponesset rental home, trees fell on roofs and roads flooded near downed power lines. It is one reported event, so we treat it as an example of wind and water arriving together, not as a pattern.

Water came in with a storm?

Stay out of standing water near wires or wet outlets, then call and tell us if it came from the bay, the roof or a window. Storm water can carry contamination, so it needs different handling.

What We Will Not Claim

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. AE and VE zones appear on the town's flood map, but no source we read ties a zone to a specific Popponesset street. The town's GIS office or your insurer can look up an exact address.

We also will not say homes here are older, newer or more likely to have basements than elsewhere. What can be said is that some do have them, so check for water below grade after heavy rain.

Rentals and Part-Year Homes

Part of Mashpee's housing, about 32 percent by the town's Housing Production Plan, is seasonal or occasional use. In a rental or second home, a small leak between guests can run unnoticed. A turnover check of ceilings below bathrooms, the water heater and under sinks catches many of them.

Two indoor culprits are covered on appliance leak water damage and water heater leak cleanup.

Safety First, Then Cleanup Steps

  1. Make the house safe: power off if water is near panels or outlets and it is safe to reach the switch, and keep children and pets out.
  2. Photograph the damage and the water line.
  3. Pull the water out using wet vacuums and submersible pumps.
  4. Dry the structure with fans and dehumidifiers, and check progress with a moisture meter.
  5. Discard materials that cannot be saved, and keep a written list.

Storm and tide water is handled under flood damage restoration. See also storm water damage and, for the wider area, Mashpee water damage restoration and New Seabury.

Insurance Notes

Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood, and flood coverage is a separate policy. Plumbing leaks and storm damage may be treated differently, and some policies add sewer or drain backup coverage. Your insurer makes the call on what it covers, so read the policy and ask before work starts.

Questions From Popponesset Homeowners

Is emergency storm water cleanup available in Popponesset?

Say whether the water came from the bay, the roof or a pipe. Roads can slow everyone in a storm and no arrival time is promised, but standing water is handled as urgent.

Is Popponesset the same as New Seabury?

They sit next to each other, and the Census lists them as separate places. We have a page for each so the details stay accurate.

Is my Popponesset home in a flood zone?

Some Popponesset shoreline may be mapped and some may not. We have not confirmed it street by street, so ask the town or your insurer before assuming either way.

Is storm water dirty water?

It can be, because it can mix with runoff or sewage. If you are unsure, treat it as contaminated, keep people out and call before cleaning it up yourself.

Can you help a rental home between guests?

Yes. Tell us the rooms, the source and the next arrival date so the plan can fit it, though drying time is set by the readings.
