Water Damage Restoration East Falmouth, MA Near Mashpee

East Falmouth is the stretch of necks, ponds and bay shore between Falmouth and Mashpee along Waquoit Bay. Each neighborhood has a different water problem.

02536

East Falmouth by Neighborhood

East Falmouth is a census-designated place with 6,278 residents in 2020 and 4,825 housing units, of which 40.4 percent were vacant, a pattern consistent with seasonal use. Within it, the necks south of Route 28 include Seacoast Shores, Menauhant, Davisville and Acapesket, and Waquoit sits at the head of Waquoit Bay.

Each area has a different relationship to the water, so the first useful question on a call is which neighborhood the house is in. A house near Menauhant Beach faces the sound. A house in Davisville sits between Green Pond and Bournes Pond. A house on Route 28 near the Davisville lights faces road runoff more than surf.

When Water Is Spreading in East Falmouth

When water is entering an East Falmouth house from a pipe, stop the supply at the main valve, stay clear of wet electrical points and phone the emergency line. Calls are taken at any hour, and Mashpee is the next town east, so crews are close.

Water from the bay, from a pond overflow or from a flooded street is contaminated. Treat it that way and do not wade through it. For storm-driven water, flood damage restoration is the service that applies.

Menauhant and the Seawall

Falmouth's hazard plan records waves overtopping the Menauhant Road seawall during Tropical Storm Henri on August 22, 2021, and coastal flooding on Menauhant Road and Surf Drive in January 2022. It also notes that erosion on western Menauhant Beach has not decreased.

The National Weather Service's Hurricane Bob page includes a photograph of East Falmouth seawall damage from 1991 and names the Buzzards Bay shore east to Cape Cod among the hardest hit areas. For a homeowner on Menauhant, the record is clear. Seawall overtopping has happened in living memory and more than once.

After any overtopping event, check crawl spaces, lower walls and garages, and photograph the water line before cleanup. Salt water also corrodes metal, so appliances and wiring that got wet deserve a qualified inspection.

Maravista, Culverts and Heavy Rain

Inland flooding is its own category. The hazard plan names localized flooding at the Maravista Avenue culvert near the Falmouth Mall entrance and records 3.75 inches of rain reported in East Falmouth in July 2017.

Heavy rain tends to expose weaknesses that dry weather hides: failed sump pumps, clogged gutters, cracked foundation walls and driveways that slope toward the house. A restoration crew dries the damage. Drainage and grading are separate fixes, and the honest advice is to address them so the next storm does not repeat the loss.

Septic and Sewage Risk Along the Necks

Falmouth's wastewater plan update says the Little Pond area, which includes Maravista, was sewered with construction beginning in 2015 and over 95 percent connected by 2019. Seacoast Shores is not yet sewered and is described as likely to be recommended in the future, and the plan recommends not sewering Davisville and Menauhant, except possibly small areas near Route 28.

That leaves several East Falmouth neighborhoods on septic for the foreseeable future. A flooded septic system or a backed-up tank is a sewage event, and it needs a plumber or septic contractor plus professional cleanup. Waquoit Bay's largest controllable nitrogen source is septic, which is why the town plans carefully about where to sew and where not to.

Landmarks and Practical Ties

The Waquoit Bay National Estuarine Research Reserve headquarters at 131 Waquoit Highway is about 3.5 miles from the Mashpee Rotary on Route 28, which gives a sense of how close crews are. Ashumet Pond straddles East Falmouth and Mashpee, and Falmouth Technology Park on Technology Park Drive is part of the commercial side of town.

For businesses and homes alike, the point is that East Falmouth and Mashpee share a shoreline and a road. If your loss involves a burst pipe rather than the shore, emergency water removal is the first step. The village next door is Falmouth, and the next town east is Mashpee itself. Drying goals and documentation follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, which any company you hire should be able to explain.

Before We Arrive in East Falmouth

Stop the water

Close the main shutoff for a pipe. For storm water, move to higher ground and keep clear.

Stay out of live circuits

Do not stand in water near outlets, appliances or the panel.

Avoid dirty water

Treat bay, pond and street water as contaminated and keep people and pets out.

Lift and bag

Raise valuables and small furniture. Leave soaked structural materials for the crew.

Photograph first

Record the water line, damaged items and the street before cleanup.

Questions From East Falmouth Homeowners

Do you cover every ZIP code in East Falmouth?

East Falmouth uses ZIP 02536, which also covers Waquoit, Teaticket and Hatchville. Neighborhoods such as Menauhant, Davisville, Seacoast Shores and Maravista fall inside it. Falmouth village (02540) has its own page.

Is emergency water removal available in East Falmouth?

Yes. Whether the water is coming off Menauhant Beach or out of a Davisville washing machine hose, extraction is available after dark and on weekends. Mashpee is the adjoining town, which helps, but we still cannot name an exact arrival minute.

Is the Menauhant area still affected by storm overtopping?

The town's hazard plan records seawall overtopping during Tropical Storm Henri in 2021 and flooding on Menauhant Road in January 2022. Ask the town which flood zone your address sits in, and read the mapped boundaries yourself.

Is my East Falmouth street on the sewer?

It depends on the neighborhood. The Little Pond area including Maravista is sewered, while Seacoast Shores, Davisville and Menauhant largely are not. Ask the Falmouth wastewater office about your address.
