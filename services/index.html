Which Service Do I Need?

Most people reach this page with a symptom, not a service name. The table below matches common situations to the page that explains them. Many jobs use more than one service in order, starting with the emergency work and ending with repair.

What you seeStart here
Water is still spreading or a pipe has burstEmergency water removal
Walls and floors are wet and need dryingWater mitigation

Storm water, surge or a flooded basementFlood damage restoration

The Five Water Damage Services

Water mitigation

Mitigation is the emergency stage. Crews stop the source, extract water, map moisture and start structural drying. Read about water mitigation for the equipment and drying goals.

Water damage cleanup

Cleanup removes damaged materials, sanitizes surfaces and clears odors. It is where the water category matters most. See how cleanup works for the three categories under ANSI/IICRC S500.

Water damage repair

Repair rebuilds drywall, ceilings, insulation and flooring after the structure is dry. Water damage repair covers ceilings, walls and floors.

Flood damage restoration

Flood work deals with storm surge, groundwater and sewage backup, all treated as contaminated. Flood damage restoration explains flood zones, FEMA maps and insurance.

Emergency water removal

Removal pumps standing water from basements, crawl spaces and garages. Emergency water removal covers burst pipes and the equipment used.

Mold Remediation as a Secondary Service

Mold usually follows moisture, so mold remediation sits alongside the water services. It starts with the leak, then containment and removal to the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard. We do not make health claims or promise that mold will never return, because both depend on the moisture source.
